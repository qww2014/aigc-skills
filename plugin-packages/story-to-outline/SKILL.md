# Story to Outline

## 子模式

- `story-development`：读取 `skills/oac-story-development/SKILL.md`
- `story-structure`：读取 `skills/oac-story-structure/SKILL.md`
- `shortfilm-five-stages`：读取 `skills/oac-shortfilm-five-stages/SKILL.md`
